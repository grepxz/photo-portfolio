Hi, I’m Hanna

I have been working as a photographer for over two years, though my story began long before. At art academy, I received classical training in the fine arts, studying composition, color theory, and art history. Later, while pursuing a STEM degree at university, I learned to look for patterns, structure my work methodically, and approach problems through observation and testing. My photography brings these two disciplines together into a single practice.

My approach centers on raw emotion, genuine reaction, natural movement, and minimal editing. I find beauty in the way people connect and interact with one another. The subject does the work; my responsibility is simply to press the shutter at the right moment.

I still have much to learn, and I believe a good photographer never settles for being good.

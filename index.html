Photography in Barcelona

I photograph weddings, events and documentary work in Barcelona and wherever else the work takes me. My approach centers on raw emotion, genuine reaction, natural movement and minimal editing.

That covers ceremonies and celebrations, corporate evenings and networking nights, sport, concerts and nightlife, and long-form journalistic projects — Pride in Barcelona, an immigration-rights protest by Houston's Mexican community, and a memorial held by Houston's Ukrainian community on the anniversary of the war.

I work in English and Spanish, and much of my client work in Barcelona is with visitors and people who have recently moved here. I travel for commissions — the documentary and event work below was photographed in Barcelona, on the Costa Brava, and in Houston, Texas.

There is also work I am actively building and shooting at reduced rates or TFP, including creative and conceptual shoots. The details are on the booking page.
